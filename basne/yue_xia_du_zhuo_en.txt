Among the flowers, a jug of wine.
I drink alone, no one close.
I raise my cup and invite the bright moon;
with my shadow, that makes three.
The moon, though, does not know how to drink,
and the shadow only follows my body around.
Still, for a while, moon and shadow keep me company:
joy has to be taken while it is spring.
I sing, and the moon drifts back and forth;
I dance, and the shadow tumbles apart.
Sober, we share the fun together;
drunk, we each go our own way.
Let us bind a friendship past all feeling
and meet again far off, in the river of stars.
